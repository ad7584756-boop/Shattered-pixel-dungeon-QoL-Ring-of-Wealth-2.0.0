# Ring of Wealth QoL 1.0.6

## Boss damage tuning

Updated direct physical boss damage for the two optional boss challenges. Values below are the base damage returned by the boss attack roll, before the target applies its normal damage reduction/resistance. Goo retains its charged/pumped-up triple-damage behavior.

| Boss | STRONGER BOSSES | OVERKILL DOOM MODE |
|---|---:|---:|
| Goo | 15 | 150 |
| Tengu | 35 | 350 |
| DM-300 | 40 | 2,500 |
| Dwarf King | 70 | 5,000 |
| Yog-Dzewa Death Gaze | 1,000 | 10,000 |

Yog-Dzewa's Death Gaze is its direct damaging special attack and uses the agreed values. Yog fists remain separate: 100,000 HP in OVERKILL DOOM MODE and at least 1,001 damage through their existing independent attack-proc rule; they do not receive the boss damage values or universal floor scaling.

The base game damage rolls remain unchanged when neither challenge is enabled.

## Build

The manual GitHub Actions workflow uploads `shattered-pixel-dungeon-qol-1.0.6-debug-apk`. This source update has not been compiled or tested in-game in this environment.
